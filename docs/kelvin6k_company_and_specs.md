## 3. Robotic Fleet & Machine Specifications

Kelvin6k develops indigenous SCARA (Selective Compliance Assembly Robot Arm) based construction robots and gantry systems:

### A. Kelvin6k Aton ("The Heavyweight Champion")
- **Category**: Industrial-Scale Construction 3D Printer.
- **Architecture**: Large-scale multi-axis SCARA robotic arm.
- **Reach & Build Envelope**: Massive reach of 10+ meters with high vertical capability for multi-story construction.
- **Payload Capacity**: High-load payload engineered to handle heavy industrial printheads, dynamic dual-component mixers, and high-volume material flow without arm deflection.
- **Target Applications**: Large-scale commercial buildings, multi-story residential housing, large infrastructure, and defense facilities.
- **Key Features**: High-precision kinematic joints, integrated steel reinforcement compatibility, continuous high-volume extrusion.

### B. Kelvin6k Atom ("Agile & Portable")
- **Category**: Portable SCARA Construction 3D Printer.
- **Architecture**: Compact, highly transportable SCARA-type robotic printer.
- **Deployment Speed**: Rapid on-site setup — ready to print in under 2 hours on any unprepared site.
- **Logistics**: Transportable in standard light commercial or utility vehicles without requiring oversize permits.
- **Target Applications**: Affordable housing, rural infrastructure, disaster relief shelters, rapid-deployment utility rooms, and mid-scale projects.
- **Durability**: Ruggedized IP-rated design engineered for harsh environmental conditions (heat, dust, moisture).

### C. Kelvin6k G-MINI ("Compact & Efficient")
- **Category**: Lightweight Mobile Prototyping & R&D 3D Printer.
- **Architecture**: Compact mobile 3D concrete printer.
- **Target Applications**: R&D facilities, university research laboratories, material testing, rapid architectural prototyping, and educational demonstrations.
- **Key Features**: High-resolution bead extrusion, intuitive digital touch control, easily moved between workstations or indoor testing bays.

### D. Gantry Platforms
- Complementary large-scale Cartesian gantry systems for precast yard operations and modular concrete component manufacturing.

## 4. Extrusion Systems & Printheads

Kelvin6k features two modular, swappable printheads engineered for specific construction demands:

### 1. Standard Printhead (Pre-Mixed Concrete Extruder)
- **Mechanism**: Single-component continuous extrusion of pre-mixed mortars and concrete mixes.
- **Aggregate Capability**: Handles concrete mixes with aggregate/grain sizes up to 8 mm.
- **Compatibility**: Compatible with concrete formulations from standard commercial ready-mix suppliers.
- **Best Suited For**: Long continuous wall segments, straight and sweeping curved walls, structural foundations, guard posts, and sanitation facilities.
- **Benefits**: Maximum volumetric throughput, high deposition rate, low operational complexity.

### 2. Accelerator Printhead (Dual-Component Instant-Hardening Extruder)
- **Mechanism**: Dynamic inline chemical dosing where a liquid accelerant is injected and mixed directly at the nozzle tip.
- **Setting Dynamics**: Flash setting and instant buildability — converts fluid, pumpable mortar into a dimensionally stable, self-supporting bead immediately upon extrusion.
- **Aggregate Capability**: Accommodates grains up to 8 mm.
- **Best Suited For**: Complex organic geometries, sharp overhangs, corbels, intricate architectural features, pillars, and artistic urban furniture without collapse or sagging.
- **Benefits**: Eliminates formwork on extreme cantilevers and complex curved profiles.

## 5. Software & Intelligence Systems
- **Proprietary Smart Slicing**: Converts complex architectural BIM/CAD models into robot-ready toolpaths optimized specifically for concrete fluid dynamics, shear rates, and layer cycle times.
- **Real-Time Weather & Slump Adaptation**: AI algorithms monitor ambient temperature and humidity, dynamically modifying extrusion flow rate and accelerant dosing to prevent cold joints or premature setting in the hose.
- **Rapid Site Scanning & Auto-Leveling**: Automatic sensor calibration compensates for uneven ground and terrain slopes before starting the print.

## 6. Sustainable Materials & Engineering Standards
- **Formulations**: Specially engineered 3D-printable cementitious mortars, fiber-reinforced concrete (FRC), zero-cement geopolymers, and mixes utilizing recycled industrial slag and pozzolanic aggregates.
- **Compliance**: Fully compliant with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications for compressive strength, flexural strength, and durability.
- **Climate Resilience**: Engineered specifically for extreme Indian environmental conditions — torrential monsoon rains, intense summer heat, and coastal humidity.
- **Monolithic Strength & Thermal Insulation**: Continuous layered printing yields monolithic wall structures with superior thermal insulation, acoustic damping, and seismic resistance compared to traditional brick-and-mortar masonry.

  - **A**: 50% faster. A structure taking 3-4 months conventionally can be printed and completed in 6-8 weeks.
- **Q: Can Kelvin6k printers construct multi-story buildings?**
  - **A**: Yes. The Aton and Atom robotic systems feature multi-meter vertical reach and integrate continuous vertical and horizontal steel reinforcement for multi-story load-bearing structures.
