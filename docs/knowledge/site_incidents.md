## Issue: Stone Stuck in Hose or Pump

**Problem:** A stone became stuck in the hose during extrusion, and was later found in the hose even when pump inspection was clear.
**Symptoms / Observation:** Blockage during extrusion; pump inspection alone did not reveal it.
**Cause:** Stone contamination in unscreened sand.
**Solution / Corrective Action:** Clean the affected flow path, inspect the pump and the hose, and screen sand with a sieve or mesh.
**Important Parameters:** None specified in the source.
**Safety / Important Notes:** Do not start printing where stone contamination risk exists without filtration. A clear pump inspection does not rule out a stone in the hose.
**English Questions:** There is a stone stuck in the hose. Why is the flow blocked by stones? Stone in the pump.
**Hinglish Questions:** Hose mein patthar fas gaya hai. Pump mein stone aa gaya. Sand mein patthar hai.
**Source:** PDF 4 record 7; Source Date 1-3 Sep 2026.
**Verification Status:** SITE-VERIFIED. Solution Type: Cleaning plus preventive filtration.
