## Issue: Layer Gap or Void

**Problem:** A local gap or void is visible in the printed concrete layers.
**Symptoms / Observation:** A section of the wall shows incomplete filling or bonding between printed material.
**Cause (POSSIBLE - NOT CONFIRMED):** Possible causes include inconsistent material flow, temporary extrusion interruption, extrusion or pump setting mismatch, material consistency and workability variation, or poor inter-layer bonding. The root cause is NOT confirmed. The source states it was not confirmed from the images alone and must be verified with the operator or technician.
**Solution / Corrective Action:** Troubleshooting to verify - check material flow, extruder and pump settings, nozzle condition, material consistency and extrusion rate. No confirmed solution is recorded; it is to be verified with the operator or technician.
**Verification:** Confirm continuous filling and bonding and stable bead geometry on subsequent layers.
**Important Parameters:** None specified in the source.
**Safety / Important Notes:** Do not present any of the above as a confirmed diagnosis. Related but distinct issues, which must be kept separate: Delamination, and Poor Layer Bonding.
**English Questions:** Why is there a gap in my printed layer? What causes voids in the wall? There is a hole in the printed layer. Layer gap or void.
**Hinglish Questions:** Layer mein gap aa raha hai. Wall mein void dikh raha hai. Material properly fill nahi ho raha.

**Solution / Corrective Action:** Troubleshooting to verify - check extrusion rate, pump pressure, nozzle height, print speed, material consistency and corner movement. No confirmed solution is recorded; it is to be verified with the operator or technician.

## Issue: Vertical or Through-Layer Crack

**Problem:** A prominent vertical crack is visible across multiple printed layers.
**Symptoms / Observation:** The crack crosses several horizontal printing layers.
**Cause (POSSIBLE - NOT CONFIRMED):** Possible causes include poor inter-layer bonding, material drying or shrinkage, excessive time gap between layers, material moisture and workability variation, or insufficient adhesion. The root cause is NOT confirmed from the images alone.
**Solution / Corrective Action:** Troubleshooting to verify - check layer-to-layer bonding, material condition, printing interval, environmental conditions, and mix workability. No confirmed solution is recorded; it is to be verified with the operator or technician.
**Verification:** None specified in the source.
**Important Parameters:** None specified in the source.
**Safety / Important Notes:** Do not present any of the above as a confirmed diagnosis. For the site-verified action on cracks, see the site-incidents record "Cracks in Printed Wall", which specifies filling the crack before shifting and inspecting print and material conditions.
